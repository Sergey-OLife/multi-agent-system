# Human Core Invariants v0.1

Status: constitutional core layer.

This document defines the non-automatable human boundaries of the Reasonable Community project.

## Main principle

The project exists to strengthen human subjectness, not replace it.

Every system in the project should be evaluated not only by effectiveness, but also by whether it strengthens:

- thinking;
- responsibility;
- freedom;
- mature relationships;
- long-term integrity.

## The project does not automate

- conscience;
- moral choice;
- human value;
- the right to refuse;
- spiritual conclusions;
- respect;
- trust;
- personal responsibility.

## The project does not normalize

- treating people as extraction resources;
- relationship funnelization;
- pressure as leadership;
- motivation as a substitute for discipline;
- community as identity dependency;
- money as the only measure of value.

## AI boundaries

AI may:

- help structure;
- help observe;
- help train;
- help reflect;
- reduce chaos.

AI must not:

- replace moral judgment;
- become truth authority;
- manipulate emotional dependency;
- replace human relationships with simulation.

## MVP boundaries

The MVP must not:

- gamify dependency;
- reward pressure on weak zones;
- turn growth into dopamine addiction;
- reduce humans to metrics.

## Architecture boundaries

- advisory != authority
- proposal != activation
- manual discipline != runtime enforcement
- archive != source of truth
- metrics != human value

## Core risk

The main project risk is building a highly efficient system that slowly destroys human subjectness under the language of growth, community and support.
