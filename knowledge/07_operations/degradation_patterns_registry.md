# Degradation Patterns Registry

Status: operational anthropology registry.

Purpose:

Track recurring degradation mechanisms inside systems of influence, MLM environments, communities, leadership structures and growth cultures.

This registry exists to identify repeatable patterns that slowly destroy:

- trust;
- maturity;
- subjectness;
- relationships;
- community integrity.

## Initial degradation candidates

### Relationship funnelization

People become viewed primarily as:

- conversion;
- structure volume;
- statistical potential;
- extraction opportunity.

Symptoms:

- every relationship moves toward pitch logic;
- refusal is experienced as resource loss;
- conversations lose non-transactional value.

### Atmosphere without spine

Communities begin punishing:

- disagreement;
- uncomfortable truth;
- skepticism;
- slow growth.

Symptoms:

- emotional conformity;
- performative positivity;
- suppression of doubt.

### Leadership dependency

Leaders become emotional centers of gravity.

Symptoms:

- inability to disagree;
- identity attachment;
- emotional obedience;
- imitation replacing maturity.

### Statistical dehumanization

Humans become interpreted mainly through:

- metrics;
- conversion;
- activation;
- retention;
- productivity.

Symptoms:

- empathy erosion;
- extraction logic;
- long-term trust collapse.

## Registry rule

Only repeatable mechanisms belong here.

Not:

- isolated emotions;
- abstract philosophy;
- decorative morality.

Only recurring patterns that can:

- damage communities;
- deform leadership;
- distort influence;
- normalize exploitation.
