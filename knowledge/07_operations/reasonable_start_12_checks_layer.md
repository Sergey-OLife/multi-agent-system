# Reasonable Start 12 Checks Layer

Status: documentation/advisory layer

Scope: book/product/MVP diagnostic layer for `Разумное сообщество`

Origin: Sergey discussion based on external prompt material adapting a wealth/mindset protocol into the book's ethical novice-route architecture.

## Purpose

This layer turns energetic novice motivation into a grounded route of practical checks.

It is not a wealth-coaching module. It is not a motivational framework. It is not a shortcut to pressure people, monetize trust, or convert a contact list into extraction material.

Its purpose is to help the book, brochure and future MVP ask:

```text
How can a person build result in MLM without losing the person in front of them?
```

The layer should convert broad ambition into:

- clear reason;
- product/service responsibility;
- ethical contact work;
- practical skill gaps;
- sane rhythm;
- decision speed;
- human boundaries;
- first safe actions;
- weekly measurable steps.

## Core law

```text
Growth is not reasonable if another person's trust becomes raw material for your plan.
```

This layer must keep the book away from two distortions:

1. passive caution that turns MLM into a fear guide;
2. aggressive achievement language that turns people into a ladder.

The right center:

```text
result + product + discipline + service + boundaries + human trace
```

## The 12 checks

### 1. Clear reason check

Question:

```text
Why am I doing this, beyond quick money, recognition or proving something?
```

Book function:

- separates reason from excitement;
- turns ambition into direction;
- prevents a borrowed dream from becoming pressure on other people.

Practical output:

- one reason sentence;
- one concrete result the person wants to create;
- one boundary the person will not cross for income.

Failure signal:

```text
The person can name income but cannot name service, responsibility or boundary.
```

### 2. Circle of sobriety check

Question:

```text
Who around me can slow me down, test my logic and tell me when I am starting to manipulate?
```

Book function:

- replaces success theater with second-eyes discipline;
- protects the novice from echo chambers;
- gives the community a conscience function.

Practical output:

- three roles: product realist, relationship realist, business realist;
- one person or search route for each role;
- one 30-minute review conversation before the next push.

Failure signal:

```text
The only surrounding voices reward speed, volume and obedience.
```

### 3. Competence map check

Question:

```text
What must I know before I deserve the trust I am asking for?
```

Book function:

- moves novice work from enthusiasm into competence;
- clarifies that product, client care and follow-up are not decorative;
- prevents premature leadership language.

Practical output:

- product basics the person can explain plainly;
- three common client situations;
- one honest limitation the person must disclose or learn around.

Failure signal:

```text
The person can repeat a presentation but cannot answer a normal client's practical question.
```

### 4. Inner noise check

Question:

```text
Which inherited phrases about money, sales, network marketing and success are steering me without permission?
```

Book function:

- gives psychological depth without turning the book into therapy;
- exposes fear, shame, greed and inherited slogans;
- helps the person act from awareness rather than reflex.

Practical output:

- three inner phrases;
- their likely source;
- a more adult counter-formulation.

Failure signal:

```text
The person calls fear wisdom or calls greed courage.
```

### 5. Cost of postponement check

Question:

```text
What is the cost of the decision I keep delaying?
```

Book function:

- brings action without aggression;
- turns vague delay into visible price;
- makes the next step smaller and cleaner.

Practical output:

- one delayed decision;
- cost of waiting;
- one minimally honest step within 48 hours.

Failure signal:

```text
The person keeps researching to avoid one normal conversation.
```

### 6. Rhythm without hysteria check

Question:

```text
What repeatable practice can I sustain without drama, self-deception or emotional violence?
```

Book function:

- replaces heroic motivation with operational rhythm;
- protects against burnout;
- keeps discipline human.

Practical output:

- weekly rhythm for learning, contact, service and recovery;
- two visible metrics;
- one recovery boundary.

Failure signal:

```text
The plan requires a new personality every Monday.
```

### 7. Problem as service entry check

Question:

```text
What problem can I help with without turning another person's weak zone into my pocket?
```

Book function:

- converts opportunity language into service language;
- preserves the law: чужая слабая зона — не ваш карман;
- teaches the novice to notice problems without predation.

Practical output:

- one real problem;
- one useful conversation;
- one honest next offer or non-offer.

Failure signal:

```text
The person is most excited when the other side is confused, afraid or lonely.
```

### 8. Normality ceiling check

Question:

```text
What level of income, responsibility and visibility feels normal to me, and where do I shrink or perform?
```

Book function:

- links money to responsibility;
- prevents income fantasy from outrunning maturity;
- helps the novice notice self-sabotage without mystical language.

Practical output:

- current normal income/responsibility level;
- next believable level;
- one practice that makes that level more normal.

Failure signal:

```text
The person wants a larger result but refuses the larger responsibility attached to it.
```

### 9. Working hypothesis check

Question:

```text
What do I need to practice as true long enough to test it honestly?
```

Book function:

- removes empty affirmation language;
- treats belief as a testable operating assumption;
- connects inner posture with outward behavior.

Practical output:

- one working hypothesis;
- one daily proof action;
- one feedback signal.

Failure signal:

```text
The person repeats phrases that do not change behavior.
```

### 10. Relational identity check

Question:

```text
Who am I becoming in relationships while I build this result?
```

Book function:

- relocates identity from image to conduct;
- keeps success from becoming costume;
- makes trust visible through action.

Practical output:

- three identity commitments;
- one behavior to stop;
- one behavior to repeat for 30 days.

Failure signal:

```text
The person becomes more impressive and less safe.
```

### 11. First safe action check

Question:

```text
What can I do in the next 72 hours that tests reality without manipulation?
```

Book function:

- prevents planning addiction;
- creates motion without pressure;
- turns reading into applied practice.

Practical output:

- one action;
- one person or situation;
- one metric;
- one stop-signal.

Failure signal:

```text
The person keeps polishing the plan because the first real step may answer back.
```

### 12. Weekly route check

Question:

```text
What are my three highest-value actions this week, and how will I know whether they were clean?
```

Book function:

- integrates the previous checks;
- creates a weekly practice loop for the brochure/MVP;
- keeps measurement tied to human quality, not just conversion.

Practical output:

- three actions;
- one metric per action;
- one ethical stop-signal per action;
- one reflection question at the end of the week.

Failure signal:

```text
The week is measured only by contacts, sales or signups, not by clarity, service or trust preserved.
```

## Book insertion points

This layer can serve several book zones:

1. Introduction / `От автора`: as a promise that the book gives a route, not a spell.
2. Early novice chapters: as first action checks after reading.
3. Product/service chapters: as competence and trust checks.
4. Contact-list chapters: as anti-predation checks.
5. Team chapters: as circle-of-sobriety and rhythm checks.
6. Leadership chapters: as identity, normality ceiling and responsibility checks.
7. Brochure route: as twelve stations of novice progression.
8. MVP: as guided diagnostic flow for a future assistant.

## MVP use

A future MVP can turn the layer into a guided flow:

Input:

- current role and experience;
- product understanding;
- personal reason;
- contact-list anxiety;
- weekly time available;
- income goal;
- fear / shame / pressure signals;
- support circle.

Output:

- current risk profile;
- one clean reason statement;
- competence gaps;
- safe first action;
- honest invitation draft;
- weekly rhythm;
- stop-signals;
- three metrics for the next week.

The MVP must not optimize for pressure, emotional bypass, fake urgency or extraction from warm contacts.

## Test scenario

Use this layer on a real chapter or novice case only in candidate mode.

Minimal test:

```text
Take one novice scene where the person wants quick result.
Run checks 1, 3, 7 and 11.
Result should produce a cleaner first action without lowering ambition or exploiting the other person.
```

Pass condition:

- ambition remains alive;
- action becomes smaller and clearer;
- product/service responsibility increases;
- human boundary becomes more visible;
- no motivational plastic enters the prose.

Fail condition:

- the output becomes a generic coaching questionnaire;
- the output moralizes instead of creating action;
- the output makes money dirty;
- the output makes people into material;
- the output kills Plotnikovsky Motor.
