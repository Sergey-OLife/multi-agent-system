## Source nerve to preserve

The source chapter is not merely a warning about goals.

Its core sequence is:

1. a person reaches a low point in MLM;
2. the question appears: where am I going and why do I need all this?;

This structure must not be treated as “what we will not write”. It must be preserved and transformed.

## Practical block title

Rejected:

- `Проверка мысли` — too psychological / therapy-workbook sounding;
- `Задание: 50 целей и одна честная причина` — reveals the conclusion too early and risks devaluing the 50-goal assignment.

Accepted working title:

```text
Практика главы: куда я иду?
```

Reason:

- it keeps the direction question alive;
- it does not prematurely moralize;
- it does not devalue 50 goals;
- it lets the reader discover the sufficient reason through the exercise.

## Accepted transition into the practice block

Use this direction:

```text
Список нужен.
Но начинать стоит не с красивых желаний, а с обычного дня, который вы хотите наконец проживать по-своему.
```

Meaning:

The chapter should not say “do not write a beautiful list”.

It should say:

- the list is necessary;
- beauty is not enough;
- the person must first connect goals with an ordinary lived day;
- then goals can be written and tested.

## Practical block sequence

Required sequence:

1. Ordinary day before the list.
2. Write 50 goals.
3. Explain why not only 10: the first ten are usually surface-level.
4. Introduce SMART as a practical filter.
5. Add the project’s maturity / human-boundary filter.
6. End with the price-boundary question: what will I not do even if it accelerates the result?

## SMART handling

SMART must be present, but not as a dry imported framework.

Use it as a practical way to make a dream usable:

- S — what exactly do I want?
- M — how will I know it is achieved?
- A — can this be reached through learning, work and system, not fantasy?
- R — is this truly mine?
- T — by when?

The `R` element is especially important for this chapter because it connects SMART with the chapter’s main question: is the goal mine or borrowed?

## Maturity / boundary check

After SMART, add the second check:

- Is this mine, borrowed or unclear?
- Am I ready to learn for this, not only be inspired?
- Can I hear refusal without resentment?
- Will I refuse to pressure close people for this?
- Does this goal make me more mature or merely prettier in other people’s eyes?
- If quick results do not come, will the reason still remain mine?
- What am I not willing to do for this goal, even if it speeds up the result?

## Chapter law

A sufficient reason is not a beautiful list of wishes.

It is a direction that survives ordinary pressure and does not permit the person to use others as material for the goal.
